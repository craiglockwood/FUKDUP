Five ways to break your audio

  • StutterTempo-synced buffer repeats from 1/2 to 1/64 notes, 2 to 16 times.
  • ReversePlays the incoming audio backwards in chunks of 40 ms to 1 second.
  • Tape stopSlows everything to a halt, pitch and all, over 50 ms to 3 seconds.
  • CrushBit depth down to 1 bit, plus sample-rate reduction.
  • GateRandom, tempo-synced chopping with density and smoothing.
  • Mix, feedback and output controls on everything
  • Locks to your DAW's tempo; every switch can be automated
  • Live visuals for each effect so you can see what it's doing

Install

Mac

  1. Download and unzip the Mac installer, then double-click FUKDUP-Installer.pkg.
  2. If macOS says it can't verify the developer, click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Follow the installer and enter your password when asked.
  4. Restart your DAW. FUKDUP appears under audio effects.

Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. In Logic, if it doesn't show up, open Plug-in Manager and rescan FUKDUP.

Windows

  1. Download and unzip the Windows file.
  2. Copy FUKDUP.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins if needed.

Needs a 64-bit DAW that supports VST3.
